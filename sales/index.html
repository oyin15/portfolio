Recommendations

  1. Shift budget from Paid Social toward Email/Referral and Organic Search. Their customers come back far more often and are worth more over time.
  2. Run a win-back campaign for the At-risk segment: repeat buyers who have gone quiet for over a year on average.
  3. Keep discounts to Black Friday and the holidays. Year-round codes cut margin and don't improve repeat buying.
  4. Fix Tableware returns with better photos, size notes and packaging.
  5. Follow up holiday buyers in January with a re-engagement offer, since they are the least likely cohort to return.
  6. Track 90-day repeat rate by channel every month as the main growth health metric.
