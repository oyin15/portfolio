Savings-maximizing

Reviewing the top 4% of transactions catches about three-quarters of fraud dollars and saves about $93K a quarter. Past that point, each extra alert costs more than the fraud it finds.

Model comparison

Time-based split: trained on Oct 2025–Jun 2026, tested on Jul–Sep 2026

PR-AUC is the honest metric here. With 1.4% fraud, ROC-AUC looks strong for almost any model; PR-AUC shows the boosted model is about 29× better than random at ranking alerts.

Recommendations

  1. Deploy the model and review the top 4% of scored transactions — about $370K in net savings a year at this volume.
  2. Step up authentication for card-not-present purchases from new devices. This is the highest-lift combination and would have blunted the spring wave.
  3. Tighten limits on gift cards and digital goods for accounts under 90 days old, where fraud runs 3–4× the baseline.
  4. Alert on monthly card-not-present fraud rate so the next wave is caught in weeks, not months.
  5. Retrain quarterly and track PR-AUC on the latest month to catch drift.
